**Sales & Profit Dashboard (Power BI)**

This project is an interactive **Sales & Profit Dashboard** built in **Power BI** using the **2024 Sample Superstore dataset**.  
The goal is to provide a single view of key business metrics across categories, regions, markets, and segments to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🔍 Key Features

- **Sales & Profit Overview**: KPIs for total sales, total profit, and profit margin.
- **Year & Category Trends**: Bar and line charts to compare yearly and category-wise performance.
- **Regional Analysis**: Region-wise profit ratios to identify high- and low-performing markets.
- **Segment Analysis**: Filter and compare Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- **Top Performers**: Top 5 salespersons by sales contribution.
- **Interactive Filters**: Slicers for year, category, region, and segment.
